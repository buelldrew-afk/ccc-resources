Daily Devotional

  • Morning and Evening
    Crossway · Spurgeon, revised by Alistair Begg
    A two- to three-minute reading twice a day. crossway.org/podcasts
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Things Unseen
    Sinclair Ferguson · Ligonier
    A short weekday devotional. ligonier.org/thingsunseen
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Truth for Life
    Alistair Begg
    Daily Bible teaching, plus a daily devotional. truthforlife.org
    Pastor Drew's pick

News & Culture

The news, from a biblical worldview.

  • The World and Everything in It
    WORLD News Group
    A weekday news podcast, plus WORLD Magazine. wng.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • The Briefing
    Albert Mohler
    Weekday news analysis. albertmohler.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Just Thinking
    Darrell Harrison & Virgil Walker
    A podcast applying biblical truth to social, cultural, political, and theological issues. Both hosts are on the pastoral staff of Redeemer Bible Church in Gilbert, Arizona. justthinking.me
    NewPastor Drew's pick

Theology, Bible & Ideas

  • Thinking in Public
    Albert Mohler
    Long-form interviews. albertmohler.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • The Bible Sojourner
    Peter Goeman
    Hard passages, theology, and culture, weekly. petergoeman.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Challies
    Tim Challies
    Daily link roundups, articles, and book reviews. challies.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Theological Studies
    Michael Vlach
    Articles on Israel, the kingdom, and the end times. theologicalstudies.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • The Cripplegate
    Nathan Busenitz, Jesse Johnson, and others
    A blog by pastors and seminary professors. thecripplegate.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • 9Marks
    Mark Dever and others
    Articles and podcasts on church health, membership, and church discipline, from a Baptist and Reformed perspective. 9marks.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Mortification of Spin
    Carl Trueman and others
    A podcast on theology, the church, and culture. Comes from a Presbyterian, covenantal setting. alliancenet.org/broadcasts/mos
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • For the Gospel
    Costi Hinn
    A weekly podcast, videos, and articles on sound doctrine, with listener Q&A. Hinn also answers the prosperity gospel, which he grew up in. forthegospel.org
    NewPastor Drew's pick
  • Redeeming Truth
    Pastors of Redeemer Bible Church, Gilbert, Arizona
    A podcast on doctrine and church life. redeemingtruthmedia.org/podcast
    NewPastor Drew's pick
  • On Preaching
    H. B. Charles Jr.
    A podcast on preaching faithfully and clearly, plus a blog and sermon outlines. Written for preachers and teachers. hbcharlesjr.com
    NewPastor Drew's pick

Apologetics & Creation

  • The Alisa Childers Podcast
    Alisa Childers
    Defending the faith, and answering progressive Christianity. alisachilders.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Stand to Reason
    Greg Koukl and others
    Apologetics podcasts, videos, and articles from the author of Tactics. str.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Answers in Genesis
    Ken Ham and others
    Articles, videos, and resources for kids on biblical, young-earth creation. answersingenesis.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Wretched
    Todd Friel
    Television, radio, and podcasts on the gospel and evangelism. Its punchy style isn't for everyone. wretched.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Justin Peters Ministries
    Justin Peters
    Teaching and videos that answer the Word of Faith movement and the prosperity gospel, including the Clouds Without Water series. justinpeters.org
    NewPastor Drew's pick

Biblical Counseling

  • Truth in Love
    Dale Johnson · ACBC
    A weekly podcast with biblical approaches to everyday problems. biblicalcounseling.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Transformed
    Greg Gifford
    A podcast and video series on anxiety, depression, relationships, and real change, from a professor at The Master's University. fortisinstitute.org/greg-gifford
    Pastor Drew's pick

Teaching Ministries

Sermons, podcasts, and articles from ministries we trust. Two come with a caveat on one topic.

  • Grace to You
    John MacArthur
    Daily radio and podcast, a sermon archive, and articles. gty.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Grace Community Church
    Sun Valley, California
    The full sermon archive of the church. gracechurch.org/sermons
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Shepherds' Conference
    Grace Community Church
    Years of conference messages for pastors and church leaders. gracechurch.org/shepherdsconference
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • The Master's Seminary
    Sun Valley, California
    Podcasts, faculty lectures, and articles. tms.edu
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Redeemer Bible Church
    Gilbert, Arizona · Jon Benzinger and the pastors
    The sermon archive of the church. redeemeraz.org/sermons
    NewPastor Drew's pick
  • Shiloh Metropolitan Baptist Church
    Jacksonville, Florida · H. B. Charles Jr.
    Sermons from H. B. Charles Jr. shiloh.church
    NewPastor Drew's pick
  • Ligonier Ministries
    R. C. Sproul, Sinclair Ferguson, and others
    Renewing Your Mind and other podcasts, articles, and Tabletalk magazine. ligonier.org
    On the end times: Ligonier teaches from a covenantal, largely amillennial framework. On Israel, the church, and the end times, go to Grace to You, Biblical Doctrine, or Michael Vlach instead.
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Desiring God
    John Piper and others
    Ask Pastor John and other podcasts, and articles. desiringgod.org
    On spiritual gifts: Desiring God is open to the continuation of the miraculous gifts. On tongues, prophecy, and the gifts, go to Grace to You, Strange Fire, or Biblical Doctrine instead.
    Pastor Drew's pick

Bible Study Tools & Archives

  • Blue Letter Bible
    Free online and app
    Bible study with Greek and Hebrew helps and commentaries. Some of the older commentaries come from other traditions. blueletterbible.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • SermonAudio
    Free online and app
    A huge library of sermons from many churches. Quality varies, so use it with discernment and stick to preachers you know. sermonaudio.com
    Pastor Drew's pick
