How We Choose

The elders are called to guard the flock and to hold firm to sound teaching (Acts 20:28; Titus 1:9). One of the most practical ways we do that is by caring about what our people read and study together. This list is meant to make that easy. We would much rather point you toward good books than spend our time warning you away from bad ones.

Every resource here has been read by Pastor Drew, sold by a bookstore we trust, or reviewed and approved. Each one fits the convictions below.

  • Scripture is our authorityThe Bible is inspired, inerrant, and sufficient for life and godliness.
  • Our doctrinal statementReformed in our understanding of salvation, dispensational in our reading of Israel and the end times.
  • What We TeachWe choose books that are in keeping with our church's What We Teach document.
  • Biblical counselingWe love the rich, Scripture-filled help that comes from the biblical counseling movement: ACBC, CCEF, and authors like Paul Tripp, Ed Welch, and David Powlison. For struggles such as conflict, anger, anxiety, and grief, these are the voices we turn to first.

Teaching Ministries

Sermons, podcasts, and articles from ministries we trust. Two come with a caveat on one topic.

  • Grace to You
    John MacArthur
    Daily radio and podcast, a sermon archive, and articles. gty.org
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Grace Community Church
    Sun Valley, California
    The full sermon archive of the church. gracechurch.org/sermons
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Shepherds' Conference
    Grace Community Church
    Years of conference messages for pastors and church leaders. gracechurch.org/shepherdsconference
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • The Master's Seminary
    Sun Valley, California
    Podcasts, faculty lectures, and articles. tms.edu
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Ligonier Ministries
    R. C. Sproul, Sinclair Ferguson, and others
    Renewing Your Mind and other podcasts, articles, and Tabletalk magazine. ligonier.org
    On the end times: Ligonier teaches from a covenantal, largely amillennial framework. On Israel, the church, and the end times, go to Grace to You, Biblical Doctrine, or Michael Vlach instead.
    Pastor Drew's pick
  • Desiring God
    John Piper and others
    Ask Pastor John and other podcasts, and articles. desiringgod.org
    On spiritual gifts: Desiring God is open to the continuation of the miraculous gifts. On tongues, prophecy, and the gifts, go to Grace to You, Strange Fire, or Biblical Doctrine instead.
    Pastor Drew's pick
